180Climate Fieldwork: A2A agent

AI can't walk into a forest. This endpoint lets another company's AI agent, speaking the open Agent2Agent (A2A) protocol, draft a field job for a person on the ground and follow it. It runs as a managed service: 180Climate reviews every draft, publishes it, invites field professionals and confirms the choice of professional with the client, and a person decides every item of evidence.

Agent Card
/.well-known/agent-card.json
Endpoint
POST /a2a, JSON-RPC 2.0, A2A 1.0 (send A2A-Version: 1.0)
Access
Invite-only pilot. Send your own client key in the header X-Fieldwork-Key; each client sees only its own tasks. Request a key at 180climate.net/contact/fieldwork.
Methods
SendMessage, GetTask, ListTasks, CancelTask
Pilot limits
50 new jobs a day and 500 in total per client

Draft a field job

Send one JSON object as a data part (or as a text part holding only the JSON):

{
  "title": "Sample-bag tag check",
  "outcome": "Photograph the tag on each soil sample bag in the store.",
  "region": "Riau, Sumatra",
  "windowStart": "2026-12-07",
  "windowEnd": "2026-12-09",
  "budgetMin": 150,
  "budgetMax": 300,
  "currency": "USD",
  "bidDeadline": "2026-12-03T18:00:00+07:00",
  "site": "Sample store A",
  "items": ["B12", "B17"],
  "checkDeadline": "2026-12-12T18:00:00+07:00"
}

Optional: deliverables, skills (up to 8), safetyNotes, photoTips (one per item). Keep the item codes and any contact details out of the texts: invited professionals see the texts, never the codes.

Follow it

Task stateWhat it means
TASK_STATE_SUBMITTEDThe draft is saved; nobody outside 180Climate can see it yet. 180Climate reviews it and publishes it.
TASK_STATE_WORKINGThe job is open: field professionals are invited and bidding.
TASK_STATE_COMPLETEDA field professional accepted, and a FieldProof field check was created. Meta's Muse Spark reads each photographed tag; a person decides every item.
TASK_STATE_CANCELEDThe job was cancelled, for example after 180Climate's review with the client.

A task reply can include a job artifact with job status, title, bid deadline, identifiers and counts, plus selection and freshness information. The artifact omits separate fields for names, prices, contact details, item codes and private links. Task status includes its last change time. If the live status cannot be read, the reply gives the last confirmed time.

Talk to a person

The task id is your job reference. To discuss a job, change it or cancel it, write to 180Climate at 180climate.net/contact/fieldwork and quote the reference.
